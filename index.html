How I Work

A process built around understanding the problem before designing the solution.

01

Research

Talk to users, audit competitors, and understand the real problem before sketching anything.

02

Define & Wireframe

Turn insights into user flows and low-fidelity wireframes, testing structure before visuals.

03

Prototype

Build interactive high-fidelity prototypes in Figma to see how the product actually feels.

04

Test & Iterate

Validate with usability testing and refine based on real feedback, not assumptions.

About Me

I design interfaces people actually enjoy using — clean logic underneath, bold personality on top.

I'm a UI/UX Designer currently working at ShellBeeHaken Ltd., based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. My work spans product design, visual UI design, and UX research — from mobile apps and file managers to travel platforms, e-commerce experiences, and landing pages. I care about the details that make an interface feel considered: motion, rhythm, and clarity.

About

Designing products
people actually want to use.

I'm a UI/UX Designer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, with 4+ years of experience designing mobile and web products that balance user needs with business goals.

Currently, I'm a UI/UX Designer II at ShellBeeHaken Ltd., and I also serve as Product Manager at KriyaKarak.

My approach starts with research — understanding what users actually need before jumping to solutions — and ends with interfaces that are both usable and visually considered.

Toolkit: Figma, Adobe XD, Photoshop, Illustrator, and a working knowledge of HTML/CSS that helps me design with development constraints in mind.

How I Work

A process built around understanding the problem before designing the solution.

01

Research

Talk to users, audit competitors, and understand the real problem before sketching anything.

02

Define & Wireframe

Turn insights into user flows and low-fidelity wireframes, testing structure before visuals.

03

Prototype

Build interactive high-fidelity prototypes in Figma to see how the product actually feels.

04

Test & Iterate

Validate with usability testing and refine based on real feedback, not assumptions.

Selected Case Studies

A closer look at a few projects — role, problem, process, and outcome.

Amazon WorkMail Redesign Concept

Role: UI/UX Designer

Problem: Amazon WorkMail's mobile inbox experience felt dense and utilitarian — this concept explores a cleaner, more modern take on core email flows like inbox, compose, and mail detail.

Process: Designed end-to-end in Figma, from layout exploration through a full set of high-fidelity UI screens.

Outcome: Published on Behance in Feb 2023, earning 157 views and 3 appreciations.

File Manager Mobile App

Role: UI Designer

Problem: Mobile file managers often bury common actions — search, sort, folder navigation — behind cluttered menus. This project set out to design a cleaner, more approachable file-browsing experience.

Process: Designed in Figma and Photoshop, covering folder views, file previews, and core navigation patterns.

Outcome: Published on Behance in Jan 2023, earning 80 views and 6 appreciations.

Travel App UI
